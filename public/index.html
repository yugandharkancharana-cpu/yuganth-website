Yuganth redesign / developer handoff

Build the shopping experience.

Review date: 6 October 2026. Source: yuganth.com homepage and product pages. This is an offline visual and interaction prototype, not a deployed replacement.

What the current site needs

  • Replace the large empty opening area and intrusive discount popup with a product-led hero and one clear shopping action.
  • Remove repeated product descriptions and brand statements. Use a concise story and structured product pages.
  • Replace product-card placeholder text “sssss”; fix repeated weight entries and product-specific enquiry copy.
  • Use clear descriptive names, visible pack size, price and stock; preserve existing customer accounts, orders and product URLs during migration.

Design system

ElementDirection
ColourChilli red #A51E29, ink #25291F, rice paper #FAF7F0, turmeric accent #EABE56.
TypographyEditorial serif headlines; clean sans-serif shopping UI. Replace system fonts with licensed brand fonts if desired.
LayoutGenerous hero, four desktop product columns, two mobile columns, simple navigation and focused cart drawer.
MotionGentle depth and tilt. Respect reduced motion. Keep shopping controls still and usable.

Three-dimensional modules

  1. Hero depth: this prototype uses the actual product image with pointer tilt and a shadow. Keep tilt within 15°; no compulsory animation.
  2. 360° pack viewer: create a textured GLB model from final packaging artwork and accurate pouch dimensions. Use a maintained model viewer or Three.js; include rotate, zoom, reset and a static image fallback. Do not fake the pack’s back or label.
  3. Product card interaction: subtle hover lift on desktop; static images on mobile. Load the full 3D viewer only on request or near the viewport.

3D asset target: approximately 1–2 MB per model after compression, 1K–2K textures and one visible viewer at a time. Test on a mid-range Android phone; fall back to WebP when WebGL fails. Drag gestures must not trap vertical mobile scrolling.

Production pages and flow

PageRequired behaviour
Home & collectionSearch, category filters, product names, pack-specific pricing and real stock status.
Product detailGallery / optional 360°, weight, quantity, ingredient and label information, origin, uses, storage, delivery check, add to cart.
Cart & checkoutEdit/remove items, stock validation, coupons, shipping, tax breakdown where applicable, secure payment, server-side confirmation and order email.
AccountSign-in, order history, tracking and addresses; preserve current customers.
Supporting pagesAbout, contact, distributors, shipping, returns, privacy and terms.

Data and assets to confirm

  • Current catalogue: Pure Gold ₹54–₹540 (shown sold out on homepage), Araku Gold ₹60–₹600, Spice Loom ₹64–₹640 and Guntur Gold ₹54–₹540.
  • Sizes shown on product pages: 100 g, 250 g, 500 g, 1 kg. This prototype estimates size prices from the displayed range; these are not verified per-variant prices. Keep Pure Gold unavailable until stock is verified.
  • Existing product photos are embedded in the file. Use higher-resolution originals and approved packaging artwork for production. No packaging redesign is implied.
  • Confirm ingredients, label declarations, nutrition, net weight, shelf life, licence details, claims, return rules, delivery charges and discount eligibility. Do not add unverified organic/certification badges or health promises.

Implementation and acceptance

The observed site uses WordPress/WooCommerce-style markup. Confirm the actual stack with the existing team. A custom WooCommerce theme can deliver this direction while retaining the backend; a headless rebuild needs a separate migration plan.

  • Map old URLs to new pages, retain product metadata and use redirects when needed.
  • Use backend pricing, stock, tax and order totals; never trust client-side totals. Verify payment webhooks and duplicate-order handling.
  • Check mobile at 360/390 px, desktop at 1440 px, keyboard navigation, screen reader labels and reduced motion.
  • Target LCP ≤2.5 s, CLS ≤0.1 and INP ≤200 ms on real mobile tests; reserve image space and avoid blocking 3D scripts.
  • Test variant switching, stock exhaustion, empty/no-result searches, cart edits, invalid coupons, postcode failures, payment success/failure and refunds before launch.

Included here: responsive homepage, image depth interaction, filters/search, weight selectors, product details, in-memory bag and checkout handoff. Not connected: backend, customer login, real payments, live stock and true GLB models.
